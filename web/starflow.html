Qualification

Green flags and red flags before launch

Use this checklist before you spend time on setup.

Green flags

  • You understand traffic, conversion, and leads.
  • You can show cases or examples.
  • You have 2-3+ people and can scale process.
  • You work with Eastern Europe or LatAm and relevant languages.
  • You respond fast and can follow scripts.
  • You understand pay-per-result and do not request upfront funding.

Red flags

  • You ask for full upfront financing.
  • You do not understand basic traffic terms.
  • You promise unrealistic volume without proof.
  • You only work with CIS/Russia GEO.
  • You refuse scripts and process.
  • You disappear or reply too slowly.
  • You focus on "guarantees" before starting work.

Discovery script

6 key questions you should answer before approval

These six points define your fit, speed, and potential payout level.

  1. What is your background (call center, affiliate marketing, recruitment)?
  2. Do you work solo or with a team? How many people?
  3. What traffic sources do you use (DMs, ads, job boards, cold outreach)?
  4. What languages do you work with?
  5. What GEOs can you cover?
  6. What questions do you have about the offer?

Why this matters

  • You show your current operating scale.
  • You prove source quality and GEO fit quickly.
  • You reduce onboarding time and launch faster.

Objections handling

Clear answers to the two most common objections

“I have no experience”

EN: We provide CRM and scripts. If you know how to reach people — you'll figure it out. The method doesn't matter, results do.

RU: Мы даём CRM и скрипты. Если умеешь находить людей — разберёшься. Способ не важен, важен результат.

  • Tell us your current experience: social, outreach, or ads.
  • Confirm you are ready to learn the process.
  • Share how much time you can commit weekly.

“Is this MLM / pyramid?”

EN: No. You don't pay to join. There is no multi-level structure. You bring candidates → they complete interviews → you get paid.

RU: Нет. Ты ничего не платишь за вход. Нет многоуровневой структуры. Приводишь кандидатов → собеседования → получаешь деньги.

  • MLM = pay to enter + level structure.
  • This model = zero entry cost + one level + pay per result.
