How this offer works

What we do

We find female candidates 18-27 for streamer positions and male candidates 18-30 for moderation roles. Streaming companies pay us for qualified interviews.

What partner does

You drive candidates to interviews by any source: ads, broadcasts, DM, mailing lists, job boards, cold outreach.

Conditions

  • CPA model: $20-40 per successful interview
  • Payouts every Sunday in USDT
  • No limits: bring more, earn more
  • We provide CRM, scripts and materials

GEO

Execution system

Channel playbooks by source

Paid Ads Launch

  • Use clear intent creatives: interview-based, not vague income promises.
  • Route traffic to qualification page, then interview booking.
  • Optimize on approved interview events, not only clicks/leads.

Job Boards Launch

  • Publish role-specific listings with clear age and GEO filters.
  • Use rapid response script to reduce candidate drop-off.
  • Track source-to-interview ratio weekly and scale best boards.

Cold Outreach Launch

  • Segment audience by geography and role intent before outreach.
  • Use short script with one CTA: pass interview screening.
  • Log response quality in CRM and iterate weekly.

Direct DM Launch

  • Start from context relevance, then move to offer details.
  • Use qualification checklist before sending booking link.
  • A/B test first two DM lines to improve response rate.
